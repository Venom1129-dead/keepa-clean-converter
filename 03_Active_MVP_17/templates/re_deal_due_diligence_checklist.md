# Real Estate Acquisition Due Diligence Checklist
> **RE AI Companion Resource — Deal Verification & Audit Protocol**

### Phase 1: Financial & Rent Roll Verification
- [ ] Certified trailing 12-month (T12) operating income statement from seller
- [ ] Current rent roll with lease expiration dates, security deposit amounts, and tenant ledger histories
- [ ] Utility bills for the trailing 24 months (water, sewer, electric, gas, trash)
- [ ] Property tax assessment history and current millage rates from county tax assessor
- [ ] Hazard and flood insurance quotes based on proposed ownership entity
- [ ] Existing service contracts (landscaping, pest control, snow removal, trash collection)

### Phase 2: Physical & Structural Inspection
- [ ] Roof inspection report: age, shingle/membrane condition, remaining useful life
- [ ] Foundation, crawlspace, and structural load-bearing inspection
- [ ] HVAC system age, SEER rating, service records, and refrigerant compliance
- [ ] Plumbing review: cast iron vs PVC/PEX, water heater age, main sewer line camera scope
- [ ] Electrical panel: breaker type (verify no Federal Pacific / Zinsco panels), knob & tube wiring
- [ ] Environmental review: lead-based paint, asbestos disclosure, radon testing

### Phase 3: Legal & Title Diligence
- [ ] Preliminary title report: verify clear ownership and identify any mechanic's liens or easements
- [ ] Zoning compliance certificate (verify current use complies with municipal zoning codes)
- [ ] Certificate of Occupancy (CO) and open building permit search
- [ ] HOA or condo bylaws, reserve study, and historical special assessment disclosures
- [ ] Tenant estoppel certificates signed by all occupying tenants prior to closing

### Phase 4: Debt & Underwriting Sizing
- [ ] DSCR lender term sheet comparison (minimum 1.25x coverage)
- [ ] Appraisal review and comparable sale analysis within 1-mile radius
- [ ] Initial capital improvement / rehab reserve escrow verification
- [ ] Final cash-to-close audit with escrow agent
