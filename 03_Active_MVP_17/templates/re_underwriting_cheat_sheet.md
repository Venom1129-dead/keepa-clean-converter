# Real Estate Underwriting Key Metrics & Benchmark Cheat Sheet
> **RE AI Companion Resource — Commercial & Residential Benchmark Guide**

| Metric | Formula | Target Benchmark | Underwriting Insight |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Net Operating Income (NOI)** | $\text{GOI} - \text{OpEx}$ | Positive & Growing | Pure unleveraged earnings generated by the property before debt service and income taxes. |
| **Cap Rate (Capitalization Rate)** | $\frac{\text{NOI}}{\text{Purchase Price}} \times 100$ | **6.0% - 9.0%** (Varies by market class) | Unleveraged rate of return. Higher Cap Rate indicates higher cash yield or higher market risk premium. |
| **Cash-on-Cash Return (CoC)** | $\frac{\text{Annual Net Cash Flow}}{\text{Total Cash Invested}} \times 100$ | **8.0% - 15.0%+** | Leveraged annual cash return on actual out-of-pocket capital deployed (down payment + closing + rehab). |
| **Debt Service Coverage Ratio (DSCR)** | $\frac{\text{NOI}}{\text{Annual Debt Service}}$ | **$\ge$ 1.25x** | Metric used by commercial banks and private lenders. Cushion above 1.0x prevents default during vacancies. |
| **Gross Rent Multiplier (GRM)** | $\frac{\text{Purchase Price}}{\text{Gross Annual Rent}}$ | **< 8.0x - 10.0x** | Quick rule-of-thumb ratio. Lower GRM indicates cheaper acquisition cost relative to top-line rent. |
| **1% Rule** | $\frac{\text{Monthly Rent}}{\text{Purchase Price}} \times 100$ | **$\ge$ 1.0%** | Screening filter: A $200,000 property should generate $\ge$ $2,000/month in gross rent to cash flow reliably. |
| **Break-Even Occupancy** | $\frac{\text{OpEx} + \text{Debt Service}}{\text{Gross Potential Income}} \times 100$ | **< 80.0%** | Minimum occupancy percentage needed to cover all operational bills and debt obligations. |
| **Operating Expense Ratio (OER)** | $\frac{\text{OpEx}}{\text{Gross Operating Income}} \times 100$ | **35% - 50%** | Industry standard benchmark for well-managed multifamily assets. Sub-30% suggests deferred maintenance. |
