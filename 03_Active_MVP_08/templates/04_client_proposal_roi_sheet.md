*Formula Framework to prevent winning contracts that pay less than minimum wage after platform fee cuts and connects bids.*

---

## 1. Unit Economics Formula
$$\text{Net Payout} = \text{Gross Contract} - (\text{Gross Contract} \times \text{Platform Fee \%}) - (\text{Connects Bid} \times \$0.15)$$

$$\text{True Effective Hourly Rate} = \frac{\text{Net Payout}}{\text{Estimated Execution Hours}}$$

$$\text{Max Break-Even Connects} = \left\lfloor \frac{\text{Gross Contract} \times (1 - \text{Platform Fee \%})}{\$0.15} \right\rfloor$$
