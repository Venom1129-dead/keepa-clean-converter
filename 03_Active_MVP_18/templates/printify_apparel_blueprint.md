## 2. Printify Direct-to-Garment (DTG) File Preparation SOP
1. **Color Profile:** sRGB IEC61966-2.1.
2. **Resolution:** Minimum 300 DPI at full print dimensions.
3. **Background Transparency:** Ensure 100% alpha transparency outside vector paths.
4. **Underbase Optimization:** All pastel and neon tones utilize high-opacity white ink underbase on dark fabrics (Pepper, Black).
5. **Print Testing:** Verify stroke weights are $\ge 1.5$ pt to prevent dropouts during DTG droplet deposition.
