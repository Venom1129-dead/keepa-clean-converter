## 2. Storefront / Directory Copy
**Title:** PC Hardening & Privacy Audit Hub — Free Interactive Windows Security & Telemetry Cockpit

**Short Description:**
Audit your PC's telemetry exposure, DNS encryption, and ransomware defense with an interactive 15-point checklist. Generates 1-click PowerShell remediation commands and downloadable markdown security reports.

**Full Description:**
Windows ships with hundreds of default telemetry probes, advertising trackers, unencrypted DNS resolvers, and open network ports that compromise your privacy and slow down system performance.

**PC Hardening & Privacy Audit Hub** helps you secure your machine in 5 minutes:
- **Live Hardening Score (0–100):** Real-time rating from "Critical Exposure" to "Hardened & Secure".
- **4 Critical Defense Pillars:** OS Telemetry, Network & Encrypted DNS (DoH), Malware & HVCI Core Isolation, and 3-2-1 Backup Readiness.
- **1-Click Remediation Generator:** Copies elevated PowerShell hardening commands directly to your clipboard for all unapplied security checks.
- **Exportable Audit Report:** Download a complete, timestamped markdown report for personal logs or corporate compliance.
