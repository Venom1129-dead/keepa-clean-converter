## 2. Storefront / Directory Copy
**Title:** RE AI Deal Analyzer Cockpit — Real Estate Cap Rate, Cash-on-Cash & DSCR Underwriting Engine

**Short Description:**
Institutional-grade real estate underwriting cockpit. Calculate Net Operating Income (NOI), Cap Rate, Cash-on-Cash Return, and Debt Service Coverage Ratio (DSCR) with 10-year pro-forma projections and 1-click printable PDF deal memos.

**Full Description:**
Evaluating real estate deals requires precision. A single miscalculation in vacancy reserve, debt service, or property management fees can turn an expected 10% return into negative monthly cash flow.

**RE AI Deal Analyzer Cockpit** provides institutional-grade underwriting in a lightweight, 100% offline client-side interface:
- **Core Underwriting Metrics:** Instant calculations for NOI, Cap Rate, Monthly/Annual Cash Flow, Cash-on-Cash Yield (CoC), and Debt Service Coverage Ratio (DSCR).
- **Lender Debt Sizing:** Standard amortizing, interest-only, and all-cash modeling with dynamic amortization schedules.
- **Dynamic 10-Year Pro-Forma:** Year-by-year projections of rent growth, expense escalation, debt paydown, and equity accumulation.
- **5-Tier Sensitivity Stress Testing:** Test severe recession, mild downturn, base case, and bull market scenarios against vacancy and inflation shocks.
- **1-Click Printable PDF Deal Memo:** Clean executive deal memo stylesheet formatted for private lenders and investment partners.
- **Zero External Dependencies:** Air-gapped, zero-CDN architecture. Runs 100% locally in any browser with zero subscription fees.
