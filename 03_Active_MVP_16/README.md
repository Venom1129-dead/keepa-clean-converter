## 1. Executive Summary
The **Nexus Community QA & Knowledge Engine** is an institutional-grade, air-gapped discussion board and lead capture system designed to aggregate and monetize organic search traffic across 4 high-value domains:
1. **Trading & Quant AI:** Indicator logic, backtesting protocols, lookahead bias prevention, DCA volatility strategies.
2. **AI & Autonomous Automation:** Air-gapped local LLM execution, multi-agent coordinator protocols, deterministic review gates.
3. **Real Estate & PropTech:** Debt Service Coverage Ratio (DSCR) sizing, Cap Rate underwriting, 1% rule analysis.
4. **Health & Performance Engineering:** Circadian sleep protocols, high-focus cognitive nutrition, mineral/electrolyte optimization.

---

## 2. Core Functional Capabilities
- **Decentralized Local Storage:** All questions, verified answers, votes, and user submissions persist cleanly in `localStorage` without external databases.
- **Dynamic Category Filtering & Live Search:** Real-time search across titles, problem excerpts, and custom tags.
- **VIP Lead Capture & Conversion Engine:** Modal lead capture for Telegram and Discord VIP channels, with 1-click export to local CSV and JSON.
- **Collapsible Solution Threads:** Interactive reply engine allowing users to add verified insights and solutions to existing discussions.
- **Zero External Dependencies:** No external CDNs, tracking scripts, or remote fonts. 100% offline air-gapped compliance.
