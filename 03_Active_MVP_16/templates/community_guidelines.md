# Nexus Community Discussion Guidelines & Quality Manifesto
> **Operational Protocol — Organic Traffic & High-Retention Engagement**

### 1. The Reproducibility Invariant
- Every code snippet or algorithmic solution posted in the **Trading** or **AI** channels must be copy-paste reproducible with deterministic outputs.
- Vague advice without equations, backtest logs, or exact parameters is flagged for revision.

### 2. Underwriting Integrity Rule
- Real estate scenarios must account for realistic operating expenses (Taxes, Insurance, Property Management, and Maintenance reserves).
- Solicitations for unvetted syndications or MLM structures are strictly barred.

### 3. Empirical Health Standards
- Health recommendations must cite peer-reviewed literature or controlled self-experiments with measurable biometric endpoints (e.g., HRV, deep sleep percentages).

### 4. Zero-Capital Commercialization
- Community moderators and contributors earn status, early access to factory software tools, and direct access to private VIP alpha channels.
